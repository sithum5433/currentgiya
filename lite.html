What’s happening at your place?

Reports need a precise device location (within 200 m). One person’s report is unverified; nearby independent reports help confirm an interruption.

How this works

This page displays community reports, not the utility’s network status. Five independent nearby reports within three minutes can confirm an outage. A missing report does not mean your supply is working.

Town selection is for browsing only. Device coordinates are kept in this tab’s memory and sent to the service to check nearby status or submit reports. They are not saved in browser storage. You can remove location permission at any time.

Lite mode uses no external fonts, maps, images or tracking scripts. Live checks pause while this tab is hidden. It requires a connection to fetch or send reports.
